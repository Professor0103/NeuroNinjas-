Neuro-Ninjas Save the Village

The village of Kagehama is in danger. Mio has developed a mysterious neurological illness: she is becoming uncoordinated, forgetful and distressed. The healer suspects it is inherited, but no one understands why symptoms appeared earlier and more severely in some of her family. You are a team of medical ninjas. Find the genetic clues, name Mio's diagnosis and find the right clinical pathway for her family.

All characters are fictional.
